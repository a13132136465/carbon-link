## Mini-DEX

The carbon marketplace adapts the price/time-priority matching rules and depth/order-entry interaction patterns from Mini-DEX (local source: `../Mini-DEX/server/src/engine/orderbook.ts` and `../Mini-DEX/web/src/components/`).
